## zoompilot source review

Review is pinned to zoompilot/zoompilot5b87a72be1489b181a12826515e0b1ed4206c7c6 and its jetlink submodule b1d704188758536e573108162d0f84c316b427d9, not a moving branch. Downloaded source blobs were checked against the Git tree. No upstream scripts were executed and no upstream implementation was installed by this review.

1. **Separate enqueue from completion waiting.** Upstream [FunctionFS sender](https://github.com/zoompilot/jetlink/blob/b1d704188758536e573108162d0f84c316b427d9/jetlink/transport/ffs.py) returns after submission and later reaps completed writes, with a1MiB outstanding-request budget and latched errors. Its comments explicitly distinguish a delayed SCHED_OTHER completion worker from a host that has not received the data. Our request_writer.c already submits native AIO, but waits for all completions before returning. This is the closest match to the observed30.891 ms completion-worker delay. Any adaptation must retain exact buffer ownership, bounded backlog, errors, deadlines and disconnect cleanup; deleting the completion wait alone is not a valid patch.
2. **Reduce physical request size.** Upstream uses8KiB requests; ours uses32KiB framed envelopes. Its [USB benchmark report](https://github.com/zoompilot/jetlink/blob/b1d704188758536e573108162d0f84c316b427d9/docs/usb-bench.md) records allocation stalls with both32KiB and16KiB requests and explains the8KiB slab-cache choice. These are upstream measurements, not our device's8KiB results. Our receiver's whole-envelope replay/CRC protection must survive a change in physical write boundaries. Do not simply replace the framing constant or assume the upstream's signal-integrity test proves our implementation.
3. **USB link power management.** Upstream [Jetson measurements](https://github.com/zoompilot/jetlink/blob/b1d704188758536e573108162d0f84c316b427d9/docs/jetson-performance.md) report an improvement with USB3 LPM disabled. Read-only inspection of the actual Jetson finds the1209:0001 device at5000Mb/s, runtime power control=on/status=active, but usb3_hardware_lpm_u1 and_u2 both enabled. Runtime autosuspend and USB3 link-state power management are distinct. This identifies a concrete A/B candidate, not a demonstrated cause. Neither setting was changed by the inspection.
4. **Memory-reclaim and logging overhead.** Upstream [root helper](https://github.com/zoompilot/jetlink/blob/b1d704188758536e573108162d0f84c316b427d9/jetlink/comma/jetlink-root.sh) adjusts dirty-page limits/extra_free_kbytes and disables FunctionFS debug-ring logging. Actual comma readback is extra_free_kbytes=0, dirty/background ratios20/10, byte limits0, and log_disable=0. These differ from upstream. VM settings affect the whole device and memory accounting; they are separate experiments, not automatic additions to the transport fix.

Receive FIFO51,16KiB reads, buffer reuse and cached UDC-state reads already exist locally. Compact outputs/GPU recurrence/native input preparation also overlap earlier local work. Upstream's Swift server, model benchmarks and TensorRT/JetPack versions differ from our N16 runtime, so its absolute latency numbers cannot be transplanted. The reviewed model-input/session paths do not demonstrate our requested N16 map-after-inference/HUD transfer scheme. That proposal remains unimplemented and untested; the earlier DIRECT_MAP=0 experiment only toggled conversion and must not be represented as deferred-map validation.

The strongest transport candidate is completion-wait separation, followed by a separately measured8KiB physical-request path. LPM, VM and debug logging are independent candidates. No new latency benefit, deployment or hard50ms guarantee is claimed from this review.
